Does any condition tell you when to sell?

A causal walk-forward on the HDB index, with mechanisms written down first: {{CONDYRS}} admitted condition-years, out-of-sample lift {{ALLLIFT}} overall and {{ALL17}} since 2017 (1.0 is no better than the base rate). Own momentum scored {{MOMLIFT}} but on only {{MOMN}} entries, {{MOMN17}} of them since 2017. Rates, rent growth and vacancy did better before 2017 and not since. Nothing meets the support rule used on the dashboard (a lift of at least 1.4 overall and since 2017, on at least 100 entries).

One unexplained pattern: September

In Bukit Panjang the quality-adjusted index rose in {{SEP_POS}} Septembers, by {{SEP_BP}} on average against {{SEP_OTHER}} in other months (t = {{SEP_T}}); September {{LASTYEAR}} was {{SEP_LAST}}. It does not show up elsewhere: other large towns average {{SEP_OTH_RANGE}}, and all towns pooled {{SEP_POOL}} (t = {{SEP_POOL_T}}). So it is a fact about one town’s past with no explanation I can test, and it is not used as a forecast.

What this does and does not say

  • No condition in the framework has told you when HDB resale prices were about to rise or fall, so the evidence supports base rates, not a call.
  • On past data the index was higher a year later in about two of three windows (median about +5%), lower in about one in three, over a wide range; the earlier flat spells went both ways.
  • The block’s premium over comparable flats has been steady, which matters more for the price a given flat might achieve than the town’s direction.
  • Nothing here models the costs of waiting or selling, loans, CPF, taxes, or the next home.
